# Earthquake Awareness

**Earthquake Awareness** is an interactive educational platform that provides special trainings and tests for students, teachers, children, and parents on what to do before, during, and after an earthquake.

## About

Earthquake Awareness is an educational web platform aiming to raise awareness about earthquakes across all segments of society:

- **Categorized Trainings**: Custom content by age and role  
- **Interactive Quizzes**: Assessments to measure learning  
- **Informative Content**: Expert-approved earthquake knowledge  
- **Emergency Guide**: Quick-access vital instructions  
- **Accessible Design**: User interface for all age groups
